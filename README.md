# Blxckroze Agent

Blxckroze Agent is a personal AI agent you own outright. Name it, give it a soul, plug in your own Groq key, and it chats with you, remembers what matters, keeps your tasks, and takes your notes. Everything it saves lives in your browser on your device. No account, no cloud, no landlord.

## Who this is for
Anyone who wants a personal AI that is actually theirs: your name on it, your rules, your data in a file you can export and carry anywhere.

## What it does
- Chat with a real tool using brain (bring your own Groq key: Llama 3.3 70B, GPT-OSS 120B, or Llama 3.1 8B)
- The agent can act, not just talk: it calls tools to remember facts, search its memory, forget on request, add and finish tasks, and save and find notes
- Memory, Tasks, and Notes tabs where you can see, edit, and delete everything it saved
- A Soul editor and an About You page, so it talks your way and knows your world before you say a word
- Export and import: your whole agent is one JSON file you own

## Why pick it
- Buy once energy: the app is one file, the key is yours, the memory is yours
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Close the page and your agent is exactly where you left it

## Get it
Open index.html in a browser. Add your free Groq key in Setup (console.groq.com) and start talking.
